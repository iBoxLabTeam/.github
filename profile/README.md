## 👋 About us

**iBox Lab Limited** is a travel-tech software company. We design and engineer products for the travel and hospitality industry, from the booking engine a traveler uses to the property system a hotel runs on every day.

Our in-house product, design, engineering, QA, mobile, and platform teams work together end to end, so what we ship is cohesive, reliable, and built for real operations.

## ✈️ What we build

| Product area | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🌍 **OTA Platform** | Online travel agency software for search, booking, payments, and customer management |
| 🏨 **PMS (Property Management System)** | Day-to-day hotel and property operations: reservations, rooms, rates, and front desk |
| 🗺️ **Trip Planner** | Itinerary and trip-building tools that turn travel ideas into bookable plans |
| 🔌 **Integrations & APIs** | Connectivity with suppliers, channels, payment gateways, and third-party travel services |
| 📱 **Mobile Apps** | Traveler and staff experiences on iOS and Android |
